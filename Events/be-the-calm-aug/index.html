Be the Calm

Ocean-side Morning Retreat — Yoga · Surf · Breakfast
WhenSat 29 August 20266:00 – 10:00am
WhereDurban Beach ClubBrowns Road

A Saturday morning to try yoga, surfing, or both. Stillness and breath as the sun comes up, then straight into the water — wetsuits provided, closing with breakfast. No experience needed, just bring yourself.

See booking options

Here's the thing about waves: we can't stop them. But we can learn to surf them. And that applies to so much more than just the ocean.

Yoga teaches you how to be still — how to breathe through discomfort, find calm in your body, anchor yourself in the present. Surfing teaches you how to go with the flow, how to read the moment, stay balanced when everything's shifting. Together, they're pure magic. You'll feel it the moment you're in the water after breathwork from your yoga practice — that body awareness, that presence of mind. Suddenly you're not fighting the ocean, you're dancing with her.

During our morning retreat, we're bringing both together. You'll start in stillness, take some time to ground and connect body and breath as the sun comes up, then jump into the ocean — one of the most refreshing starts to a morning (and yes, don't worry, you'll have a wetsuit) — and leave feeling alive and relaxed at the same time, genuinely proud of yourself for trying something new. Because there's something beautiful about showing up as a beginner, allowing yourself to not be perfect, and just having fun along the way.

Winter here in eThekwini is truly the best. While everyone else in Joburg and Cape Town is cold and miserable, we get to still spend glorious days on the beach. And we're making the most of it.

This isn't about becoming an expert by 10am. It's about spending a morning doing something that scares you a little, feels good in your body, and reminds you that you're capable of more than you think. You'll leave here feeling like something shifted — proof that you can do uncomfortable things, and have fun while you're doing it.

Let's go.

Your guides

Meet your guides for the morning.

Magdarie van Staden

Yoga

Magdarie has been teaching yoga for over 10 years and holds her 500hr Yoga Alliance certification. Her classes are warm and easy to drop into, whatever your experience — more about feeling good in your body than getting the posture right.

Tash Whittaker

Surf

Tash's life revolves around the rhythm of the waves. As an accredited surf coach and qualified lifeguard, she believes that catching waves is simply a byproduct of a much greater experience. Instead of just focusing on mechanics, she takes a different approach by helping surfers build a meaningful, lifelong relationship with the ocean. Her goal is to help you understand the water's energy, read its subtle cues, and develop a deep, enduring love and stoke for the ocean to really enjoy your surfing. Patient, encouraging, and genuinely excited to share this connection with you.
