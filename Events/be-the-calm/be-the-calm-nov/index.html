Here's the thing about waves: we can't stop them. But we can learn to surf them. And that applies to so much more than just the ocean.

Yoga teaches you to be still. To breathe through discomfort, find calm in your body, anchor yourself in the present.

Surfing asks you to go with the flow, read the moment, stay balanced when everything's moving.

Put them together and you are creating magic.

This is not about becoming an expert in one morning. It's about doing something that scares you a little and feels good in your body anyway. Something that reminds you what you're actually capable of. And just perhaps you learn something about yourself: that you can do the uncomfortable thing and still have fun doing it.

Let's go!

Your guides

Meet your guides for the morning.

Magdarie van Staden

Yoga

Magdarie has been teaching yoga for over 10 years and holds her 500hr Yoga Alliance certification. Her classes are warm and easy to drop into, whatever your experience. It's less about getting the posture right, more about feeling good in your body.

Tash Whittaker

Surf

Tash's life runs on the rhythm of the waves. She's an accredited surf coach and qualified lifeguard, but for her, catching a wave is really just the byproduct of something bigger. Rather than drilling technique, she helps surfers build an actual relationship with the ocean, one that lasts well past the lesson. Patient, encouraging, and genuinely stoked to share it with you.
